# Renew a domain on GoDaddy
1. Open https://dcc.godaddy.com/control/portfolio and sign in with the owner's saved GoDaddy sign-in.
2. Find {domain} in the list and open it.
3. Choose Renew, pick 1 year, and go to checkout.
4. STOP and ask the owner to approve the total before paying.
5. After payment, open the domain again and read the new expiry date.
Report: the new expiry date and the order number.
